Our Story

More Than a Boutique

Lophix was born from a simple belief: every woman deserves to feel extraordinary. Nestled in the heart of Opebi, Ikeja, we've been Lagos' most trusted destination for premium women's fashion since 2018.

From international designer labels to carefully curated ready-to-wear, our collection speaks to the multifaceted modern woman — professional, elegant, bold, and always beautifully herself.
